A Life Beyond
Boundaries.

Osho was an Indian spiritual teacher, philosopher and speaker. His teachings explored meditation, awareness, love and the freedom of human consciousness.

Born on December 11, 1931, Osho became known for his unconventional approach to spirituality and meditation. His talks covered subjects ranging from human relationships to individual freedom and self-awareness.

Osho began his academic career, teaching philosophy at universities in India.
