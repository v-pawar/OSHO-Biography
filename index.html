GET TO KNOW HIM

A Life Beyond
Boundaries.

Osho was an Indian spiritual teacher, philosopher and speaker. His teachings explored meditation, awareness, love and the freedom of human consciousness.

Born on December 11, 1931, Osho became known for his unconventional approach to spirituality and meditation. His talks covered subjects ranging from human relationships to individual freedom and self-awareness.

HIS LIFE STORY

The Journey
Of OSHO.

Explore the important milestones and chapters of Osho's remarkable life journey.

1931
THE BEGINNING

Birth in Kuchwada

Osho was born on December 11, 1931, in Kuchwada, Madhya Pradesh, India.

1953
SPIRITUAL AWAKENING

A Life-Changing Experience

At the age of 21, Osho described experiencing enlightenment in Jabalpur.

1957
ACADEMIC CAREER

Teaching Philosophy

Osho began his academic career, teaching philosophy at universities in India.

1974
A NEW BEGINNING

Pune Ashram

Osho established his meditation community in Pune, Maharashtra.

1981
INTERNATIONAL JOURNEY

Journey to Oregon

Osho moved to the United States, where his followers established a community in Oregon.

1985
RETURN JOURNEY

Leaving America

Osho left the United States and eventually returned to India.

1990
FINAL CHAPTER

January 19, 1990

Osho passed away in Pune, India. His teachings and books continue to reach readers worldwide.
